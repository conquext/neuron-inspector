## Anti-patterns

Actions that should never be taken autonomously:
- Never auto-send replies to complaints or negative feedback
- Never auto-accept requests from accounts with zero posts/followers (likely bots)
- Never reply to obvious template/mass outreach — archive silently
- Never engage with political or controversial content
